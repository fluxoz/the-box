The Configurator

Set up your Box before it first boots.

Answer a few questions and this page builds your orders into one of the two ways a machine becomes a Box: a flash image, or one pasted command. Everything runs locally: nothing you enter leaves this browser.

01Identitywhat it answers to

The name the Box announces on your network: reach it at <name>.local.

Auto derives a stable per-unit name like box-3f2a1b, so several Boxes never collide on one network.

02Networkhow it gets online

Plug in a cable and it comes up on DHCP. Nothing to configure.

Written to the unit as a saved connection on first boot. WPA/WPA2 personal.

03Accesswho can operate it remotely

SSH public keys authorized to manage the Box. Password login is disabled: keys only. Leave empty to manage from the local console and web dashboard.

04Public Exposureoptional

The Box and its dashboard stay on your network. Make services public later, from the dashboard, whenever you want.

Bring your own tunnel: paste a token from Cloudflare Zero Trust and the Box comes online already reachable from the internet: no open ports, no traffic through us. Point the tunnel's hostname at localhost:2693.

05Target Diskwhat gets erased

Picks the largest internal (non-removable) disk. USB sticks are never touched, so the installer stick itself is safe.

Use a stable /dev/disk/by-id/… path; it survives reboots and re-cabling.

06Confirm & Deploypoint of no return
This ERASES the target disk.
Every file on the selected disk is permanently destroyed and replaced with Box OS. That consent rides inside the orders below, and the installer refuses to run without it.
