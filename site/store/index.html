The fine print

What these machines really do.

They are mixture-of-experts machines. The big open MoE models (gpt-oss-120b, the Qwen3 family) run at conversation speed. Dense 70B models do not run well on anything in this class, whatever the listing says. If someone quotes you 70B numbers on unified memory, ask for the tokens per second.

How the guarantee works. Every machine is benchmarked on our bench before it ships, and its numbers ride along in the box. If it does not hit its rating in your hands within 30 days, we refund your full deposit and arrange the hardware return. Ratings are set below published community benchmarks for this exact hardware on purpose.

What the price is. These are standard mini PCs; you can price them anywhere, and you should. Our total is the hardware at what it actually costs us (the distributor's invoice comes with your quote) plus a flat 15 percent ($99 minimum) for the bench work, the setup, and the support. The deposit on each card is that fee at the current price band (re-minted when prices move); the hardware is billed when you approve the quote. Totals on this page are checked nightly against the makers' own stores.

No subscription required. If you never pay us again, the machine keeps working, keeps updating, and keeps its models.

The hardware warranty is the manufacturer's, including onsite service where they offer it. The software is open source, and the install path is tested nightly against the live site (last night's timing is on the front page).
