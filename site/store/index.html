How pricing works

You pay what we pay, plus a flat fee. That's the whole trick.

The honest part

What these machines really do.

They are mixture-of-experts machines. The big open MoE models (gpt-oss-120b, the Qwen3 family) run at conversation speed. Dense 70B models do not run well on anything in this class, whatever the listing says. If someone quotes you 70B numbers on unified memory, ask for the tokens per second.

No subscription required. A Box that never pays us another dollar keeps working, keeps updating, and keeps its models. The paid parts are conveniences, never a leash on your own machine.

The hardware warranty is the manufacturer's, including onsite service on configurations that offer it. The Box software is ours, it's open source, and every install is proven nightly by a machine, not a claim.

Free & open source. No account, no phone-home. Prices float with the memory market; the $499 doesn't.
