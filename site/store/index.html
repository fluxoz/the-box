Use cases

What these machines are for.

A private ChatGPT for the household

The open-webui preset plus gpt-oss-120b: chat on every phone in the house, and nothing typed into it ever leaves the machine.

An unmetered endpoint for coding agents

Anything that takes a base URL (Continue, Cline, opencode) points at the Box's /v1. Agents can think all night; the bill is electricity.

The API-bill killer

Summarizers, classifiers, batch jobs run against your own endpoint. Ten thousand documents costs about a dollar of power.

Questions for documents that can't leave

The qdrant preset plus document chat: contracts, patient notes, client files. Your files never touch anyone else's computer.

Automation with a brain

It's also your server

Push-to-deploy for your projects, PR previews, your own domain, encrypted backups. One machine does all of it.

The fine print

What these machines really do.

They are mixture-of-experts machines. The big open MoE models (gpt-oss-120b, the Qwen3 family) run at conversation speed. Dense 70B models do not run well on anything in this class, whatever the listing says. If someone quotes you 70B numbers on unified memory, ask for the tokens per second.

How the guarantee works. Every machine is benchmarked on our bench before it ships, and its numbers ride along in the box. If it does not hit its rating in your hands within 30 days, we refund our full fee and arrange the hardware return. Ratings are set below published community benchmarks for this exact hardware on purpose.

No subscription required. If you never pay us again, the machine keeps working, keeps updating, and keeps its models.

The hardware warranty is the manufacturer's, including onsite service where they offer it. The software is open source, and the install path is tested nightly against the live site (last night's timing is on the front page).
