Updates & rollback

The whole platform (boxd, the base OS, the template library) is one tracked channel. An update is one more atomic generation, and a failed one rolls itself back.

Update

Dashboard → System:

  • Check for updates: compares your pinned platform to the channel.
  • Update now: rebuilds the system on the new release, switches, and health-checks.

Or from the CLI over SSH:

boxd channel check      # is there a new release?
boxd channel update     # apply it (no-op if already current)

Turn on auto-update and a timer does the same on a schedule.

Health-checked rollback

Every update builds a new generation and switches to it, then checks the Box came up healthy. If it didn't, the Box rolls back automatically and restores the previous pin. A bad release doesn't strand your Box, and doesn't get retried next time. You can also roll back any generation by hand from Generations.

This is the same mechanism your own service changes use. Nothing about a platform update is special. It's a generation, and generations are reversible.

Track a fork or a pin

By default a Box tracks the official platform (github:fluxoz/the-box). Point it at a fork, a mirror, or a pinned revision instead with boxd channel set. That's useful if you want to review releases before they reach your fleet.
