Guide

Backups

Box Backup is client-side encrypted and points at storage you own. Everything is encrypted on the Box before it leaves, so your storage provider (and we) only ever see ciphertext.

What gets backed up

The backup set is derived from your Box's manifest, not a list you maintain: your config, secrets, and auth, plus each service's declared state directory. Add a stateful service and its data is protected automatically.

The Nix store and past generations are deliberately not backed up. They rebuild from the (backed-up) config. Backups stay small and are about your data, not the OS.

Bring your own backend

Point it at storage you control:

BackendUse
localAnother disk on the Box, or a mounted drive.
s3Backblaze B2, Cloudflare R2, MinIO, any S3-compatible bucket.
sftpA NAS or another server over SSH.
restA restic REST server.

Turn it on

  1. Dashboard → Backup → choose a backend and enter its details.
  2. The Box generates your backup key and shows it once. Save it. It's the encryption key, it never leaves the Box, and without it a backup can't be restored by anyone.
  3. Pick a schedule (e.g. daily). An hourly heartbeat runs a backup when one is due.
The key is the whole game. Zero-knowledge means exactly that: lose the key and the data is gone. Put it in your recovery kit.

Restore

Dashboard → Backup shows your snapshots. Restore everything, or scope it to a single service's data or just the Box's config. On a fresh Box, re-enter the same backend and key, and the snapshots are right there.

Managed offsite (optional)

Don't want to run storage? The managed tier gives you metered, encrypted-in-flight-and-at-rest offsite storage in one command. It's still zero-knowledge, and the key still never leaves your Box:

boxd cloud enroll --server <url> --token <one-time-token>

It links the Box to a cloud account, provisions scoped storage, and points Box Backup at it. The software is free; only the operated storage is paid.
