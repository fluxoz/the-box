Service catalog

100 services your Box can run, each one a single container the Box configures for you: the right port, its own storage, sane exposure, and generated credentials where the app needs them. Deploy any of them from + Deploy in the console, or over MCP with deploy name=<yours> template=<id>.

Every entry here was deployed on a real Box and checked the way you would check it: the app's own page answering through the Box's front door, or the engine answering its own protocol. Where something needs hardware the Box cannot pass through yet, or a config file it cannot write for you, the entry says so rather than half-working.

Reach: proxied is served on your network by the Box (and can be published to the internet through your tunnel); internal is reachable only by your other services on this Box; exposed opens its own port on your network for protocols that are not the web.
