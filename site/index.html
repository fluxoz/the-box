Point a spare machine or a cheap VPS at one command. It wipes itself, installs a self-managing appliance OS, and comes back as a Box you run from your browser — declarative, atomic, and yours.

$ curl -fsSL https://thebox.build/install.sh | sudo sh

On a running Linux box or VPS. It kexecs into the installer — no reboot menu.

Wipes every disk and installs Box OS. Bring settings from the Configurator, or decide on the box.

> irm https://thebox.build/install.ps1 | iex

In an elevated PowerShell. Stages the installer from Windows and takes the machine over.

Wipes every disk. See the Windows guide.

Blow-away install

No dual-boot, no cruft. One generic image; your machine's identity arrives at first boot.

Atomic generations

Every change is a new generation. Roll back instantly; a failed health check rolls back itself.

Decide on the box

Pick your disk layout — single, mirror, or pool — at the screen or in a browser, with the real disks in front of you.

Sovereign by default

Your keys and secrets never leave your browser or your box. No account, no phone-home.
